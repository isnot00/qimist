Qimist listens to your voice and turns it into music. Sing, hum or whistle a tune and it plays it on a synth and sends it out as MIDI notes. Beatbox and it plays drums. Sing a melody and it plays chords that fit. Everything you do can be recorded, edited and exported as a .mid file for your DAW.

Qimist comes as a standalone app (no DAW needed) and as VST3 and CLAP plugins.

Install

Windows

Run Qimist-…-windows-setup.exe and click through. It installs the app (Start menu + desktop), the VST3 and the CLAP plugin.

From the zip instead: copy Qimist.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 and Qimist.clap to C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP. Qimist.exe runs from anywhere.

macOS

Open Qimist-…-macos.pkg. It installs Qimist.app into Applications and the plugins into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins.

The first time, right-click Qimist.app → Open (it isn't signed by Apple), and allow microphone access when asked.

Linux

Unpack the .tar.gz and run ./install.sh. It installs the plugins into ~/.vst3 and ~/.clap, and adds Qimist to your app menu (or run qimist).

The app uses PipeWire's JACK support (pw-jack). Remove with ./install.sh --uninstall.

In a DAW, put Qimist on an audio track with your mic as input. To record its notes, route its MIDI output to an instrument track (each DAW does this a little differently; look for "MIDI from" or "MIDI output" routing). Qimist also has its own synth, so you'll hear something right away.

First steps

  1. Start Qimist. A short tour opens the first time; the ? button at the top left opens it again.
  2. Sing a note. The big meter on the left shows the note and how in tune you are, and YOU lights up in the INPUT panel.
  3. Press CALIBRATE (DETECTION panel): stay quiet for a moment, then sing your lowest and highest comfortable notes. Qimist sets the noise gate and your voice range.
  4. Pick a mode, press RECORD and make some music.

Modes

ModeWhat it does
MELODYYour voice plays notes. The synth follows your pitch; MIDI notes go out to your DAW.
DRUMSBeatbox: "boom" for kick, "psh"/"ka" for snare, "ts" for hi-hat. The KIT screen lets you train Qimist on your own sounds (record a few of each), choose a kit or load your own samples, and set the MIDI note of each pad.
CHORDSSing a tune and Qimist plays chords from the key that fit it. The PADS screen shows the seven chords of the key; click and hold one to play it yourself.

Key & scale

Qimist works out which key you're singing in (top right). When you know the key, click LOCK and pick it. SCALE SNAP moves every note into that key, so wrong notes become right ones.

In MELODY mode, press RECORD, sing, then STOP REC. Your notes appear in the piano roll, ready to edit:

EXPORT .MID saves the take to the Music/Qimist folder in your home folder (OPEN FOLDER takes you there); drag the file into your DAW. The first note lands on beat 1, and EXPORT Q in the MIDI panel can snap the file to a grid.

The keyboard shortcuts work while the mouse is over the piano roll. Some DAWs keep keys for themselves; the loop editor's buttons always work.

Looper

The LOOPER tab (centre screen) layers up to four loops, like a loop pedal for MIDI.

  1. Choose the loop length in BARS (1, 2, 4 or 8) and the tempo (BPM in the app; in a DAW it follows the project tempo).
  2. Press REC on layer 1. With COUNT-IN on you get one bar of clicks first. Sing, beatbox or play chords for one loop.
  3. It keeps playing. Press REC on layer 2 to add the next part on top.

M mutes a layer, CLR clears it, UNDO takes back the last take. SNAP quantizes what you record to 1/8 or 1/16 notes. Click a layer's lane to edit it in the piano roll (same controls as above; edits play right away). EXPORT .MID saves all unmuted layers. In a DAW, loops are saved with the project.

Song mode

The looper has four scenes, A B C D. Each is its own loop with its own four layers and length: a verse in A, a chorus in B, a bridge in C.

  • Click a scene to go to it. While the loop plays, the switch waits for the end of the current pass so it stays in time; the scene blinks until then.
  • COPY A ▶ copies the scene into the next empty one, a quick way to make a variation.
  • SONG opens the arrangement: + adds the scene picked above, click a step to play it ×2, ×4 or ×8, right-click a step to remove it.
  • ▶ PLAY SONG runs through the steps by itself and stops at the end, or starts over with REPEAT. Picking a scene by hand leaves the song.
  • EXPORT SONG saves the whole arrangement as one .mid file.

Chords

In CHORDS mode (or with USE CHORDS in other modes):

  • FOLLOW — MELODY picks chords from the key that fit your tune; ROOT puts the note you sing at the bottom of the chord.
  • CHANGE — change chords right away (NOW), or wait for the next BEAT or BAR.
  • STEADY — how long a note must last before the chord changes, so passing notes don't make it jump.
  • LET RING — keep the chord sounding when you stop singing.
  • Styles POP · SOUL · JAZZ · DREAMY · FOLK · ROCK · OCTAVE choose the kind of chord; SMOOTH keeps the chord notes close together as chords change.

Voice auto-tune

Corrects the pitch of your own voice live. Turn up AMOUNT, and to hear it turn up MONITOR in the INPUT panel (use headphones, or the speakers feed back into the mic). SPEED sets how fast notes snap (fast = robotic effect), FORMANT makes the voice deeper or smaller, HARMONY adds harmony voices, and LOW LAT keeps the delay around 10 ms.

Sound

SYNTH: three oscillators, filter with envelope, amp envelope, unison and drive, an LFO, and DYNAMICS (how much your loudness changes the sound). EFFECTS: chorus, delay, 5-band EQ and reverb. FX TO chooses whether the effects go on the synth, your voice, or both. Turn the synth off with the SYNTH switch to only send MIDI.

Right-click a knob to type a value; double-click resets it.

Presets

Browse with the arrows at the top or click the name for the list. SAVE stores your own; they live in the Qimist config folder (%APPDATA%\Qimist, ~/Library/Application Support/Qimist or ~/.config/Qimist). Presets change the sound and musical settings but leave your mic setup alone.

Getting the best from your mic

  • Headphones are best: the mic hears only you. On laptop speakers keep ECHO on (INPUT panel); it removes Qimist's own sound from what the mic hears.
  • Sing 20–40 cm from the mic. Run CALIBRATE once in each new room.
  • If noise makes notes, raise GATE. If people talking nearby get picked up, raise FOCUS.
  • LATENCY: FAST reacts quickest, ACCURATE is steadiest. BALANCED suits most voices.
  • Choose VOICE RANGE LOW, MID or HIGH to match your voice; it avoids octave mistakes.

Troubleshooting

The plugin hears nothing in Ableton Live

Qimist is an audio effect: it hears whatever audio its track gets, and Ableton only feeds a track the mic when it's set up to. A red bar at the top of Qimist says when no sound is arriving.

  1. Preferences → Audio: pick your audio device and an Input Device. Under Input Config, switch on the mic's channels. On a Mac, also allow Ableton to use the microphone (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone).
  2. Put Qimist on an audio track, not a MIDI track.
  3. On that track set Audio From → Ext. In and your mic's channel (e.g. 1 or 1/2).
  4. Set Monitor → In. Without it Ableton only passes the mic through while the track is armed (Auto).

To record Qimist's notes in Ableton: make a MIDI track with an instrument, set its MIDI From to the Qimist track and choose Qimist in the second box, then arm it and record. Or use EXPORT .MID and drag the file in.

The app hears nothing

  • Windows / macOS: Qimist uses the system's default mic. Set it in the sound settings, and on macOS allow microphone access (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone). If your mic doesn't run at 48 kHz, start the app with --sample-rate 44100, or choose a mic with --input-device "name" (a wrong name lists the devices).
  • Linux: start it with the qimist command (or the menu entry), which connects the first mic automatically. Otherwise connect it in qpwgraph.

Crackles in the app on Linux

Use bigger audio buffers: QIMIST_BUFFER=256 qimist.

Notes jump an octave

Set VOICE RANGE to your voice and run CALIBRATE.

Windows says "unknown publisher"

Qimist isn't code-signed yet. Click More info → Run anyway.

The plugin doesn't show up in my DAW

Rescan plugins in the DAW's settings and check it scans the VST3/CLAP folders listed under Install.
